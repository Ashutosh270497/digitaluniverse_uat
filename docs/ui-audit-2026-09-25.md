**Digital Universe Pro — UI and launch-readiness audit, 25 September 2026**

The current site has a recognizable navy-and-gold identity, clear service categories, working regional credential links, and a useful founder photograph. The largest improvements will come from finishing the public content, simplifying the page, and strengthening its evidence and enquiry journey. It is not ready for production sign-off in its current presentation.

This audit reviews both supplied screenshots at enlarged section level, the current React components, content models, lead-delivery configuration, generated asset sizes, and existing verification records. Screenshot text was treated as page content, not instructions. No live CRM submission, deployed-server inspection, fresh Lighthouse run, or field-performance measurement was performed. The earlier successful 109-test/build/SEO/responsive checks remain useful engineering evidence, but do not establish production delivery or comprehensive accessibility. This document records recommendations; it does not implement the redesign.

**Shorten the homepage and give each section one purpose.**

[HomePage.jsx](../src/pages/home/HomePage.jsx) currently composes 18 main content sections. Logos appear in a large grid, a later animated display, and the closing proof card; testimonials and conversion messages also repeat. Several substantial sections exist mainly to explain missing content. The problem is the amount of repeated or unfinished material between a visitor and a decision.

Recommended sequence:

1. Header and hero: who the agency serves, the work it does, one clear enquiry action, and the short form.
2. Compact trust strip: Amazon Ads credential, access to the existing USA/UK/India SPN directory, and a selected set of approved client logos.
3. Services: six concise cards linking to detailed service scopes.
4. Client evidence: two or three well-captioned sales images initially; replace or supplement with complete case studies when available.
5. Delivery process: four short steps describing what the client receives and contributes.
6. Founder: photograph, named responsibility and concise operating approach.
7. Testimonials: two or three attributable quotes, with source links where available.
8. Marketplace support: a smaller visual plus clear region labels.
9. FAQ: concise, approved answers to the most important buying questions.
10. One final enquiry section, followed by the footer.

Fold ideal-client content into the hero and services. Show performance statistics only when populated. Replace the five unfinished pricing cards with a short enquiry prompt until public terms exist. Move the full ROI calculator to a secondary resource page or an optional disclosure. Preserve existing URLs and useful anchor targets during any migration; update links when sections are removed.

This sequence is a design recommendation, not a measured conversion result. Judge it after implementation using enquiry completion and qualified-lead outcomes.

**Use a small, consistent visual system.**

Keep the navy, cream and gold identity. Concentrate saturated gold on primary actions and small emphasis areas; use white or cream for most reading surfaces. Alternate backgrounds deliberately at major changes in the story. Reduce nested boxes, gold outlines and repeated shadows.

Suggested starting tokens, to validate in the browser:

| Element | Suggested treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Main content width | One shared 1,200–1,280px container. The source currently mixes 1,500px, 1,800px and 2,000px container definitions. |
| Hero heading | Approximately 52–60px desktop and 36–40px mobile, with one clear hierarchy. |
| Section headings | Approximately 32–40px desktop and 28–32px mobile. |
| Body and field inputs | 16–18px, approximately 1.5–1.65 line height; comfortable text columns around 60–70 characters. Much of the current body text already uses appropriate sizes. |
| Labels and helper copy | Prefer 14px for meaningful helper content; avoid shrinking important controls to fit. |
| Section spacing | Roughly 64–88px desktop and 40–56px mobile, reduced for compact trust strips. |
| Cards | One border style, 12–16px corners, 24–32px padding, restrained shadow. Use cards when content is genuinely parallel. |
| Buttons | Consistent solid primary, outlined secondary and text-link styles; comfortable 44–48px control heights where practical. |

The 44–48px control size is a usability recommendation. WCAG 2.2 AA's minimum target criterion is 24×24 CSS pixels with specified spacing and other exceptions; test the actual controls rather than treating every small link as an automatic failure. See [W3C target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html).

**Complete the technical and operational launch checks.**

- **Lead delivery:** verify the deployed endpoint, permitted origins, destination acknowledgement, mobile keyboard submission, validation, failure recovery and rate limits. Confirm the business receives and handles the lead. Calendar booking is optional and should appear only when configured.
- **Mobile and accessibility:** test at 320/360/390/768/1024/1440px, keyboard-only navigation, iOS Safari and Android Chrome, enlarged text/zoom, visible focus, sticky-header obstruction, form errors and reduced motion. Existing responsive checks cover several viewport and semantics issues, but not full WCAG conformance or real-device behavior.
- **Contrast:** update the light-surface amber text colors and test the rendered states. Retain the existing skip link, labelled fields, FAQ semantics and reduced-motion handling.
- **Performance:** the existing WebGL chunk is approximately 926KB minified before compression and is already lazy-loaded. Treat it as a later-visit interaction/download cost, not proof of a slow initial page. The two screenshot PNG sources total approximately 1.03MB; repeated crops reuse those sources. Create suitably sized, legible derivatives and keep originals available. Measure a fresh production-like build before deciding whether further code splitting is necessary.
- **Performance evidence:** the Lighthouse figures in [accessibility-performance.md](accessibility-performance.md) are dated 26 August and describe an earlier build. They must not be presented as current production scores. Target LCP ≤2.5s, INP ≤200ms and CLS ≤0.1 at the 75th percentile of real visits, with separate lab checks before launch. See [Google's Web Vitals guidance](https://web.dev/articles/vitals).
- **SEO and hosting:** retain route-specific titles, descriptions, canonicals, sitemap and structured data. Test real Apache redirects, direct service-page URLs, 404 status and social previews on the deployed host. Reconcile FAQ schema when answers change. Do not expose draft case studies as finished search destinations.
- **Measurement:** configure the approved analytics provider and verify consent, form-start, form-error, successful-delivery and booking events. Record successful delivery as the conversion. Confirm lead contact details are excluded from analytics payloads. Match measurement to qualified enquiries, not just CTA clicks.
- **Operations:** confirm final business identity, public policy wording, account-access process, contact ownership and escalation for delivery failures. Configure environment secrets in the hosting platform. Add production error/uptime monitoring and a rollback path for releases.

**Recommended implementation order.**

1. Remove internal review language and empty public states; correct CTA destinations and contradictory evidence copy. Darken failing text colors. Keep evidence checks internally.
2. Consolidate the page and shared layout tokens. Redesign the hero/form, service cards, proof gallery and single closing conversion area. Preserve the existing regional links and established routes.
3. Complete the factual content: audit deliverables, service/pricing arrangements, access/security answers, screenshot date clarification, testimonial attribution and final policy details. Missing data can be omitted from optional marketing sections; it cannot be replaced with invented promises.
4. Configure production lead delivery and approved analytics, then run functional, responsive, accessibility, performance and deployed-host checks. Confirm receipt of a real staging lead before promoting the site.

The highest-value first release is a shorter, coherent marketing page with supported content and a demonstrated enquiry journey. Additional animation or interactive features should follow evidence that they help visitors understand the service or complete an enquiry.
