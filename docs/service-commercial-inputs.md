# Service and commercial inputs still required

## Purpose

The homepage now explains six Amazon service categories, the four delivery stages, and the questions a prospect should resolve before granting access or signing a proposal.

The service descriptions are scope directions, not universal packages. The signed proposal must identify the exact ASINs, marketplaces, deliverables, responsibilities, reporting, exclusions, commercial terms, and measurement rules for the engagement.

## Commercial decisions requiring approval

The repository does not contain approved answers for:

1. Whether each service is sold as fixed-fee, monthly retainer, performance-based, or a documented combination.
2. The minimum engagement period, renewal structure, notice period, and cancellation terms.
3. The specific variables that affect price and how each variable changes the fee.
4. The standard onboarding timeline and the conditions that start that timeline.
5. An approved starting price or realistic range, including currency and tax treatment.
6. Whether Amazon media spend, creative production, external tools, travel, translation, or other pass-through costs are included in or separate from agency fees.
7. Payment schedule, invoicing, late-payment, pause, refund, and scope-change rules.

## Service-delivery decisions requiring approval

For each of the six services, provide:

1. Standard reporting frequency, delivery format, review-meeting cadence, owner, and response expectations.
2. The default deliverables and the conditions that change them.
3. Service-specific exclusions.
4. Supported ASIN or SKU volumes and marketplace limits, if any.
5. Required client roles, approval turnaround, and escalation contacts.
6. The Seller Central and Amazon Ads permission matrix.
7. Supported tools, exports, dashboards, and communication channels.
8. The line between advisory work, implementation, Seller Support coordination, creative production, translation, tax or compliance support, logistics, and inventory responsibility.
9. Eligibility requirements for A+ Content, Brand Story, Storefront, Brand Registry, launches, and international expansion.
10. A verified operational list of Amazon marketplaces supported for ongoing work.

## Security and ownership decisions requiring approval

Before requesting client access, provide an approved written position covering:

1. Least-privilege access, credential handling, multi-factor authentication, access reviews, and offboarding.
2. Data storage locations, retention, deletion, backups, approved tools, subprocessors, and incident response.
3. Whether sensitive Seller Central or advertising data may be downloaded and where it may be processed.
4. Seller Central account control and ownership.
5. Ownership, licence, export, and handover of campaigns, copy, creative files, reports, dashboards, and working documents.
6. Confidentiality and permission requirements for case studies, testimonials, logos, and screenshots.

## Measurement decisions requiring approval

For every proposal, define:

1. Baselines, targets, source systems, time windows, currency, and comparison-period logic.
2. Which measures apply: ACoS, TACoS, revenue, conversion rate, organic sales share, account-health issues, operational completion, or other approved measures.
3. Revenue and advertising-spend definitions.
4. How inventory, pricing, promotions, seasonality, reviews, product changes, and off-Amazon activity are considered.
5. Who reviews and approves performance reporting.
6. Confirmation that targets are objectives rather than guaranteed outcomes.

## Current case-study limitation

No service currently has a publishable relevant case study. See `docs/case-study-evidence.md` for the evidence and permission required before associating a case study with a service.
